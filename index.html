Gameplay

🚗 Gesture Drive

An open-world driving game you steer with your hands.

  • Hold both hands up like a steering wheel
  • Tilt the wheel left / right to steer
  • Right index finger up = gas
  • Left index finger up = brake / reverse
  • Collect 🪙 coins, hit the blue ⚡ pads for a speed boost
  • Hit the orange 🛫 ramps — the red strips give you a boost too!

Sit about an arm's length from the camera, in a well-lit room. Turn your sound on 🔊
